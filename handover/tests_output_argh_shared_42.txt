**Avoid Unintended Categories of Tests**

1. Clearly Identify Project Types and Scope:

Consider the purpose, structure, and intended users of your project to ensure you cover all relevant types and aspects.

2. Include All Possible System Interaction Modes:

Don't just test primary functionality; consider other ways users interact with your system, including edge cases and security vulnerabilities.

3. Enumerate All Functionalities in Context:

Break down the project into smaller chunks of functionality and address each chunk through a combination of theory and practical checks.

4. Engage with Multiple Disciplines:

Invite feedback from different roles to ensure your project covers all aspects, incorporating software development team members, quality assurance experts, and designers.

5. Document and Review the Process:

Maintain records of discussions and decisions about issue handling and test coverage, including meeting minutes, documentation, and version control logs.

6. Implement Continuous Improvement:

Regularly analyze and adapt your methodology to improve its effectiveness, including regular code reviews, automation where possible, and reflection on past issues.

**The goal of this updated methodology is not only to increase the quality of tests but also to ensure that no functional requirement or edge case goes unaddressed.**
